Teacher directions

Share this page for student choice or copy an investigation’s link for a group assignment. Use Projector view on the projected browser. Plan about 35 minutes per lab and allow additional time for the class share-out. For a 75-minute period, two 35-minute investigations leave about five minutes for transitions, so the synthesis can follow the next day.

Each group exports an evidence file containing simulated readings only. On the synthesis page, import one or more group files, or enter results from oral reports. Lab and synthesis drafts are saved in this browser. Export a report to submit through your usual classroom workflow.
